Notes for Dev 7 (#748) and Dev 5 (#778)

  1. Show the limit before the picker (CPO, #744): "2 of 3 videos left this month · MP4 or MOV, up to 50 MB and 60 seconds". At 0 left the picker still opens for photos and video rows say the month is used. A video counts only once it's Ready; a refused, failed or removed-before-ready one doesn't use one of the 3.
  2. Length cap: I propose 60 s. It is Stories' own per-card cap, so one clip fits every placement; the Analyst's Tricity sample (#1858) is a median ~31 s. At 50 MB the size is usually what bites first (a 1080p Android clip is ~60–130 MB a minute), so the too-big line computes a target: floor(duration × 50 / size / 5) × 5 seconds ("about 25 seconds"). Hide the number if it'd be under 5 s and say "a shorter part" instead. CTO can change 60; the words take {max}.
  3. Too big / too long / wrong type are red with one fix, never "Use anyway". Fit (wide 16:9) is amber and can be added: "Feed only". Size and type need no decoding; length comes from the browser's <video> metadata. If the phone can't read it, that is a refusal ("Save it as MP4 and try again"), not a pass.
  4. Upload is one request on the Free plan (no resume), so progress shows MB, and the keep-open line says the honest thing: if the page closes, it starts again. Keep the beforeunload ask while a video is uploading.
  5. "Getting it ready…" = finalize plus the poster frame. The tile shows the poster as soon as the browser has it, isn't pickable until Ready, and after 60 s gets the still-going line (polite live region, no focus move). Meta's own processing at launch is #749's state, not this one.
  6. Check unavailable fails closed (CPO, Nov 2): amber "couldn't check just now · Try again", no "Use anyway". This supersedes the photo frame on #1222 for mandatory checks; only an optional helper may offer it.
  7. Sheet title and button follow the picks: "Add photos & videos" / "Add 2 videos" / "Add 1 photo and 1 video". The sheet's button is blue on #1973 today; use the leaf primary (bg-primary) like the rest of the app.
  8. Words go to #1868's EN/HI keys; the HI here is a draft for the CPO's words check. Rendered at 390 and 360 in both: no overflow, no target under 44 px, no button text on two lines.
