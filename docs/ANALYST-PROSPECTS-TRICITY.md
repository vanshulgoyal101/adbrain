## Outreach guardrails

1. Re-open Ad Library links and record exact live count, format and “started
   running” date before contacting anyone; the count is volatile.
2. Use only the business's published page/site/WhatsApp route. Do not copy a
   staff member's name, personal number or email into the CRM.
3. Ask permission before sending a pilot link, and keep the first offer free
   with the owner's approved ad-account budget and a paused launch.
