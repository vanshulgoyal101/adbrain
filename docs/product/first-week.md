# Your first week after Go live

Part of [Features and workflows](README.md).

This page starts where [Your first ad on a phone](first-ad.md) ends: you tapped Go
live and saw "You're live. Meta is checking your ads; they usually start within a
day." For every screen in detail, see [Manage](manage.md).

## Day 1: Meta checks your ads

- **You see:** on Home, "Meta checks your ads first. This usually takes a few
  hours, sometimes up to a day." Nothing to do yet.
- **Where to look:** on **Campaigns**, each campaign shows Meta's answer for its
  ads: "In Meta's review", "Approved by Meta" or "Rejected by Meta".
- **Still nothing after a day?** The campaign says "Not showing yet" and "Your ads
  haven't started showing after a day. Check what Meta says." Tap "See Meta's
  status" to open Meta Ads Manager.
- **If Meta says no:** see [An ad is rejected](#an-ad-is-rejected) below.

## Days 1 and 2: what's normal

- Few or no chats at first is normal. While it is early, the campaign says "Early
  days". With no chats yet but ads showing, it says how many times they were shown
  and "No chats yet; many people message in the evening."
- AdBrain doesn't judge a campaign until it has 5 chats and ₹500 spent. Until then
  it says, for example, "2 chats so far. AdBrain will tell you if it's working
  after 5."
- Meta also takes time to learn who replies. Each new run starts that learning
  from zero, so "results may be slow at first". Changing who sees a running
  campaign can mean a fresh copy that goes back to Meta's review, and "learning
  restarts". So give it time before you change things.
- Meta spreads your money across the days: "one day can be more than ₹300
  and another less. The total never goes past the limit above."

## Each day: check Home, reply fast

- **Home** shows your results so far: "Chats" (or "Enquiries" if you run a form
  ad), "Spent" and "Per chat". "—" means not yet. Below it: "Updated 4m ago".
- Above the numbers is one line on how it's going, such as "Each chat costs ₹45."
  If people are waiting, it starts with "1 person is waiting for your reply." and
  shows "Reply now".
- **Chats come to your WhatsApp.** Customers who tap your ad message you there.
  "The messages are in your WhatsApp Business app." Reply there, and reply fast:
  "people often pick the first business that answers."
- **Enquiries** (open the menu, then "Enquiries"; or "See enquiries" on Home): form enquiries arrive on their own,
  WhatsApp chats only when you add them. For a WhatsApp ad, tap "Add an enquiry"
  for each real one; not every chat is a customer. Home may ask "Which of your 6
  chats were real enquiries?" with "Add enquiries".
- On an enquiry with a phone number, tap "WhatsApp them" (it "Opens WhatsApp with:"
  a ready first reply) or "Call". Home then shows "You usually contact people
  within" a time, so you can see how fast you are.
- **Someone bought?** Tap "Mark customers" on Home, then "Became a customer" on
  that enquiry. For a WhatsApp chat you didn't add, tap "Got a customer? +1". Home
  then shows what each customer cost in ad spend.
- **One ad costs much more than the others?** AdBrain may say so and offer "Pause
  this ad". "Paused [ad]. The other ads keep running."
- **Each week:** Home shows "Last week: …" with last Monday to Sunday's chats,
  real enquiries and spend.

## An ad is rejected

- **You see:** on Home and on the campaign, a line such as "Meta didn't approve
  your ad." with the reason in plain words, for example "The headline reads like
  clickbait to Meta." Then "Your other ad is running." or "Nothing was spent."
- **Tap** the button under it. It matches the reason:
  - "Edit the words": change the words Meta objected to.
  - "Make a new picture": opens Create ads.
  - "Check your WhatsApp number": when "People who tap can't reach you".
  - "Ask Meta to review again": opens Meta Ads Manager.
  - "Message us": when AdBrain can't fix it, for example "Ads for property, loans
    or jobs need a special Meta setting AdBrain doesn't support yet."
- If Meta gave no clear reason, you see "Meta didn't say exactly why. Here's their
  message:" with Meta's own words.

## Pausing and turning back on

- **One campaign:** on **Campaigns**, tap "Pause". You see "Campaign paused."
- **Everything at once:** on Home, tap "Pause all ads", then "Pause all". "Nothing
  is deleted, and nothing more will be spent once Meta stops them (usually within
  minutes)." When done: "All 2 campaigns are paused. Nothing more will be spent."
  If one fails, tap "Try again" or "Open in Ads Manager".
- **Turn it back on:** on **Campaigns**, tap "Resume". AdBrain checks the campaign
  first, then you confirm. After a pause of 7 days or more, Meta's learning starts
  again, so results may be slow at first.

## What the money lines mean

- **Daily budget:** the "₹300 a day" you picked. Meta spreads it, so one day can
  be a little more and another less.
- **Run total:** "Stops at ₹708" is the most this run can cost, for example
  "₹300 a day for 2 days, ₹708 at most including Meta's GST". It never goes past
  that.
- **Meta's GST:** Meta adds 18% GST to your ad spend; the total above includes it.
- **How Meta takes the money:** "Card: Meta charges it after the ads run. UPI or
  net banking: add money first, at least ₹708, or the ads stop when it runs out."
- **Your ad money goes to Meta:** "Your Page, your ad account, your card. AdBrain
  never holds your ad money."
- **AdBrain itself:** "Your first month of AdBrain is free. After that it's ₹999 a
  month; we'll remind you before it's due." Near the end Home says "Your free
  month ends on" a date and "Then it's ₹999 a month. No auto-debit; you pay when
  you choose." When due: "Your ₹999 plan payment is due" and "Running ads keep
  running. New ads and campaigns wait until you pay."

## When the run ends

- Meta stops the ads when the days you picked are over, and never spends past
  the "Stops at" total.
- **Run it again:** when nothing is running, Home says "Nothing is running. Run
  your best ad again?" with "Run it again". Or, if the campaign on **Campaigns**
  shows "Run this again", tap it. It "Copies it into a new campaign, best ad first. Nothing goes to Meta until
  you review and send it, paused."
- **Something new:** tap "New campaign" on Home. You go through the same three
  steps again, with a new offer if you like.
- After your first week Home may ask "One question after your first week: would
  you pay ₹999 a month to keep using AdBrain?" Your answer helps us.

## When to message us

- Message us if an ad is rejected and the button says "Message us", if your ads
  are "Not showing yet" after a day and Meta's status doesn't explain it, or if a
  pause doesn't work.
- **Where:** open the menu and tap "Help on WhatsApp", or "Help" at the
  top of the setup screen. These show only once AdBrain's support number is set
  up; until then, message the person who gave you AdBrain.
