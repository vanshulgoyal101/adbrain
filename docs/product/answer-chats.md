# Answer the chats your ad brings

Part of [Features and workflows](README.md).

Your WhatsApp ad has done its job when someone messages you. Whether they become a
customer depends on how fast and how well you answer. This page shows what the
customer saw before they wrote, what their first message looks like, and three
answers you can save today.

## What the customer saw

1. **The ad**, on Facebook or Instagram, with a button that opens WhatsApp.
2. **A chat with your business**, opened in their WhatsApp. At the top is your
   welcome. AdBrain's suggestion is "Hi! Thanks for messaging [your business]. Send
   this message and we'll reply here." in the ads' language (English, Hindi,
   Hinglish or Punjabi).
3. **A message typed for them**, ready to send, such as "Hi! I'd like a free site
   survey." AdBrain writes it from your offer and area: "Hi! I'd like the [offer] in
   [city]." or "Hi! I'd like to know more about [your business]." They tap send, or
   change it first.
4. **Or questions to tap**, if you chose "Quick questions" instead: "Tap a question
   below and we'll reply here." with up to three questions such as "What's the
   price?".
5. **A "Call now" button**, if you chose it. "Calls go to the WhatsApp number on
   your ads."

Before you go live, Review shows this under "When they tap", for example
"Customers see your welcome and 'Hi! I'd like a free site survey.' ready to send.",
with a preview of the chat. Change the words there before you launch.

The number printed on the ad picture is the phone from your business details. The
chat opens the WhatsApp number linked to your Facebook Page. Until
[#2274](https://github.com/vanshulgoyal101/adbrain/issues/2274) makes them match,
watch both: chats arrive on the Page's WhatsApp number, and calls from the picture
go to your business phone.

## The first message

The first message is usually the ready message, as it was typed for them, or the
question they tapped. So you already know what they want: your offer. What they
ask next is nearly always price, place or time. The chat is in your WhatsApp
Business app, not in AdBrain.

## Three answers to save

Save these as quick replies in WhatsApp Business (Settings → Business tools →
Quick replies), then change the words in brackets to yours. Each answer gives a
fact and asks one thing back.

**Price** (shortcut `/price`)

> Thank you for writing! [Service] starts at ₹[amount]. The exact price depends on
> [size or need]. Can you tell me [what you need], so I can give you the right
> price?

**Location** (shortcut `/where`)

> We're at [address or area], near [landmark]. We also come to you in [areas you
> serve]. Which area are you in?

**Timing** (shortcut `/when`)

> We're open [days], [hours]. The earliest slot is [day and time]. Shall I book it
> for you?

## Answer within 5 minutes

People message two or three businesses from ads and often pick the first that
answers. Your cost per chat counts every chat, answered or not. A chat that waits
an hour is money spent with little chance of a customer.

- Keep WhatsApp Business notifications on during your open hours.
- Send the saved answer first, then the details.
- If you can't answer properly now, send one line saying when you will.

## Where chats show in AdBrain

Home counts them as "Chats" and shows "Per chat". When someone is waiting, it says
"1 person is waiting for your reply." with "Reply now". Open "Enquiries" and tap "Add an enquiry" for each chat that
was a real customer question, so AdBrain shows what a real enquiry costs. The full
daily routine is in [Your first week after Go live](first-week.md#each-day-check-home-reply-fast).
