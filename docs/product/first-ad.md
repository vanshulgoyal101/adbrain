# Your first ad on a phone

Part of [Features and workflows](README.md).

Before you start, have these ready: a Facebook Page for your business, a Meta ad
account with a way to pay (a card, or UPI or net banking), and the WhatsApp number
customers should message. AdBrain also asks what you sell, where you serve and one
offer. Nothing spends until you tap Go live, and you can stop at any step.
"Your first month of AdBrain is free. After that it's ₹999 a month; we'll remind
you before it's due."

## Before you start: get Facebook ready (once, about 15 minutes)

Do this on your phone, signed in to Facebook as yourself (the owner), a day before
if you can. Meta's own screens; nothing here spends money.

1. **Send AdBrain your Facebook profile link** on WhatsApp (Facebook › your profile ›
   ⋯ › Copy profile link). While Facebook is still reviewing AdBrain, we add you as a
   tester of the adBrain app, and you can only connect after that.
2. **Accept the invite:** open developers.facebook.com/requests and accept adBrain.
   Facebook may ask you to register as a developer first: it's free and needs a code
   sent to your phone.
3. **A business portfolio:** open business.facebook.com. If it asks you to create a
   business portfolio, use your business's name, your own name and your business
   email. Already have one? Use it. Never use someone else's portfolio.
4. **Your Page in it:** Settings › Accounts › Pages › Add › Add a Page, and choose
   your business's Facebook Page. Your Page should have a WhatsApp button with the
   number customers should message.
5. **An ad account in rupees:** Settings › Accounts › Ad accounts › Add › Create a
   new ad account. Currency **Indian Rupee (INR)** and time zone **Asia/Kolkata**:
   Meta can't change these later. Choose "My business" when it asks who it's for.
   Already have an ad account in rupees? Add it here instead.
6. **A way to pay:** in that ad account, Payment settings › Add payment method: a
   card, or UPI or net banking with money added first (at least what your run
   costs; AdBrain shows it, for example ₹708).

Then sign in to AdBrain (below). On Go live you'll connect this Page and ad account.

## If someone from AdBrain set it up for you

On a visit, the person from AdBrain may make your business details and ads on
their phone, then type your email to hand them to you. No link or message is sent:
you sign in yourself, on your own phone, within 30 minutes.

- **Tap:** open adbrain.vanshul.com/login, then "Continue with Google" or "Email
  me a sign-in link", using the same email you gave them.
- **You see:** "Make [your business] yours?" and "AdBrain set this up for you on
  today's visit: your business details and 3 ads. Making it yours signs everyone
  else out of it." Tap "Make it mine". If it isn't yours, tap "This isn't my
  business".
- **Then:** Home says "It's yours. The 'Sample' mark is off your ads. Nothing runs
  until you tap Go live." with "Your 3 ads are ready" and "Connect and go live".
  Carry on from [Step 3: Go live](#step-3-go-live). They can't have connected
  Facebook or made a campaign for you, so you connect your own Page and ad account.
  You pay Meta for the ads yourself, on your own card or UPI.
- **If it fails:** a team email gets "Sign in with the owner's own email, not a
  team account." If your email already has an AdBrain business (say you signed
  up before the visit), you see "This email already has an AdBrain business; use
  another email or ask AdBrain". After 30 minutes the hand-over ends; ask them to
  start it again.

## Sign in

- **You see:** on a phone, a card first: "From your business to WhatsApp chats".
  Tap "Skip" (or "Create my free account") to reach the form, headed "Create your
  free account". Already have an account? Tap "Already have an account? Sign in".
  The "Sign in" link at the top of the homepage skips the card and opens "Welcome back".
- **Tap:** "Continue with Google", or type your email and tap "Email me a sign-in
  link". "No password needed. The link creates your account."
- **Takes:** a minute. With email you see "Check your email": open the link on the
  same phone. "It works once, for a short time."
- **If it fails:** the screen says, for example, "Could not send a sign-in link.
  Please try again." Tap "Send again", or use "Continue with Google".

After signing in you land on "Set up your first ad", "Step 1 of 3". The bar at the
top shows your three steps. If you leave and come back, Home shows "Your first ad"
with "Continue"; once a picked ad matches your current details it shows "Your ad is
ready" (or "Your N ads are ready") with "Connect and go live". If your picked
ads were made from older details or in Studio, Home takes you back to Your ads instead.

## Step 1: Your business

- **You see:** "Tell us about your business" and "Five things. Your ads are made
  from these, and you can change them any time.": "Business name", "What you sell",
  "Where you serve", "Your offer" and "WhatsApp number".
- **Tap:** "Make my ads". If you have a website, type it first and tap "Fill it
  from my website"; no website? Tap "No website? Paste a bio or listing".
  Check "Your ads will be in" (the language), and if you like fill "Why do
  customers pick you? (optional)": one line makes your ads sound like you.
- **Takes:** about a minute. Your answers save as you type ("Saved").
- **If it fails:** a field you missed is named in red, for example "Add at least one
  city or area." or "Enter a 10-digit mobile number." If your website can't be read
  you see "We couldn't read that website. Fill it in yourself; it takes about a
  minute."

"Making ads is included. Nothing goes live until you say so."

## Step 2: Your ads

- **You see:** "Making your ads…", then lines such as "Writing 3 ads in English…",
  "Making the pictures…" and "Almost ready…". When done: "Your ads are ready".
  AdBrain has picked the strongest; tap an ad to change your pick ("Tap to pick" / "Picked"), or the
  pencil to change its words.
- **Tap:** "Continue with 1 ad" (the number matches how many you picked).
- **Made ads before?** You may see "Make ads from your details": "Your earlier ads
  were made from older details or in Studio, so they aren't used here. Making ads
  is included." Tap "Make 3 ads" for fresh ones, or "Use your earlier ads".
- **Takes:** "Usually about a minute"; up to about 2 minutes when the picture
  services are busy. Nothing is published at this step.
- **If it fails:** making pictures uses free picture services that are sometimes
  busy. Then some ads may not arrive: "1 ad couldn't be made." with "Make another",
  or none: "We couldn't make your ads this time." with "Try again". Wait a minute,
  then tap it. If you see "Your ads are still being made. Check again in a minute;
  we won't start another batch.", wait and tap "Check again". Nothing is spent.

## Step 3: Go live

- **You see:** "Go live", with your picked ads, the **Budget** and the list
  "Before you go live":
  - Budget: choose "₹300 a day" (or ₹500, ₹1,000) and "2 days" or "7 days". The
    line above sums it up: "₹300 a day for 2 days, ₹708 at most including Meta's GST".
  - "Where": the areas your ads show in.
  - "When they tap": "They message you on WhatsApp at …" (your number), with the
    first message they get, in quotes.
  - "Facebook Page": "Not connected" until you connect it.
  - "Payment on Meta": "Add a card, or pay by UPI or net banking". "Card: Meta
    charges it after the ads run. UPI or net banking: add money first, at least
    ₹708, or the ads stop when it runs out." Tap "Add payment in Meta" to do it
    on Meta.
  - "Stops at ₹708": the most this run can ever cost.
  - "Your Page, your ad account, your card. AdBrain never holds your ad money."
- **Tap:** while Facebook is still reviewing AdBrain, the Facebook Page row says
  "Facebook is still reviewing AdBrain. Until then, your Facebook account needs our
  invite: open your invites and accept adBrain, then tap Connect." Tap "Open my
  invites", accept adBrain, come back, then tap "Connect Facebook Page". A Meta window opens: tap "Connect
  Business", sign in to Facebook, choose your Page and ad account, tap "Use
  selected business", then "Continue to review". The row now says "Connected"
  and the button becomes "Go live · ₹300 a day". Tap it.
- **Takes:** about 10 minutes the first time, mostly connecting Facebook. After
  you tap Go live you see "Checking your campaign…" (AdBrain picks the age range
  and audience for your ads, as **Recommend audience** does in Campaigns, up to
  about a minute), "Creating it on Meta, paused…", then "Turning it on…". AdBrain
  creates the campaign paused first and only switches it on once everything checks out.
- **If it fails:** nothing is spent when something fails. What the screen says:
  - Meta connect: "Meta has not finished yet. Complete the Meta window, then check
    again. Your draft is saved." Finish in the Meta window, then tap "Check again".
    "Didn't connect? If Facebook said the app isn't available, accept the invite
    first, then try again.": you skipped the invite above. Tap "Open my invites",
    accept, then "Try again".
  - No ad account or Page shared: "Meta shared no ad account with AdBrain…" (or
    "no Facebook Page"). Tap "Reconnect Meta" and tick the ad account and Page in
    Meta's window. No ad account yet? Create one in rupees in the business portfolio.
  - Not in one portfolio: "This Page and ad account aren't in the same business
    portfolio…". If another pair is listed as ready, choose it. Otherwise tap "Open
    business settings", add the Page and the rupee ad account to one portfolio,
    then tap "Reconnect Meta".
  - Ad account not active: "This ad account is not active. Check Account quality
    in Meta." Fix it on Meta's Account quality page, or connect another ad
    account.
  - No way to pay: "Meta needs a way to pay first: a card, or money added by UPI or
    net banking. Add it in Meta, then tap Go live again. Nothing was spent." Tap
    "Add payment in Meta", then come back and tap Go live again.
  - Something in the campaign needs fixing: the reasons are listed with "Fix in
    campaign review". Tap it, fix what it names, and go live from there.
  - Saved but not switched on: "Your campaign is saved, paused. Nothing was spent."
    Tap "Open Campaigns" to turn it on from there.
  - Changed while checking: "This campaign changed after it was checked. Nothing
    was spent. Tap Go live again."

When it works: "You're live. Meta is checking your ads; they usually start within a
day." Tap "Open your campaign" to see it.

## After it's live

- "Meta checks your ads first. This usually takes a few hours, sometimes up to a
  day."
- Customers who tap your ad message you straight on your WhatsApp. Reply there,
  within 5 minutes: [Answer the chats your ad brings](answer-chats.md) has three
  answers to save.
- **Home** shows your results: "Chats" (or "Enquiries"), "Spent" and "Per chat".
- **Enquiries** (open the menu, then "Enquiries"; or "See enquiries" on Home): form enquiries arrive on their own;
  WhatsApp chats show there once you add them. On each one tap "WhatsApp them" for a ready-written first reply, or "Call". Home
  shows how quickly you usually contact people, so reply fast.
- Did someone buy? Tap "Mark customers" so you can see what a customer costs.
- To stop: on **Campaigns**, tap "Pause" on the campaign; to stop everything at
  once, tap "Pause all ads" on Home, then "Pause all". "Nothing is deleted, and
  nothing more will be spent once Meta stops them (usually within minutes)." Tap
  "Resume" to turn a campaign back on.
- The run stops by itself at the total you chose. To run again, tap "New campaign".
- What to expect day by day: [Your first week after Go live](first-week.md).
- Stuck at any step? Tap "Help" at the top of the setup screen to message us on
  WhatsApp. (It shows only once AdBrain's support number is set up; until then,
  message the person who gave you AdBrain.)
