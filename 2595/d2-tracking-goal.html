Notes for Dev 2 (D2), after C2's DTO

  1. 1Brand card "Website tracking", shown only when Meta is connected and Brand has a website; it sits after Contact details. One radio row per pixel: name, last event, and a ✓/! line for Lead and Purchase over the checked window. Footer: Check again (44 px) + Events Manager ↗ link + "Checked 14:05 · last 7 days". brand-form.tsx is 613 lines; put the card in its own brand-tracking-card.tsx. The words say "tracking"; "pixel" only where Meta shows it.
  2. 2none: Meta's guide is the fix; name Shopify, WooCommerce and WordPress. AdBrain never installs anything.
  3. 3unreadable means "we couldn't check", never "another business owns it" (CTO). The share advice is conditional ("If another business manages…").
  4. 4Goal chips are a radio group, 3 equal columns (fits HI at 360). Visits is the default and is always available. A goal whose tracking isn't ready stays in the row as aria-disabled with "Needs tracking" under its name, so the owner sees it exists; tapping it focuses the reason below. Never auto-switch to it or away from it.
  5. 5The reason uses the same state words as Brand (event_missing here) and one button, Finish tracking setup → /brand#tracking. With no tracking at all, one combined line covers both goals.
  6. 6The CPO's sales line shows only for Sales, directly above Make my ads, as information (not amber). No budget change, no goal switch.
  7. 7Review "Tracking" appears only for signups/sales: what Review pinned (pixel + event, checked time). Button line comes from the ads' own words (#2703), so it says "Buttons:" when ads differ.
  8. 8Blockers: tracking none/unreadable/quiet/event_missing → Check tracking (re-reads; the body shows the window and checked time). Goal ↔ button mismatch → Review ads. Send stays off until both clear.
  9. 9"Run as website visits instead" is a link-style button: it changes the goal to visits and re-runs Review. Nothing falls back on its own.
  10. ✓Rendered EN 390 and HI 360: no overflow, every target ≥ 44 px, no button text on two lines. HI is a draft for the CPO's words check; add keys only (campaigns.goal.*, brand.tracking.*).
